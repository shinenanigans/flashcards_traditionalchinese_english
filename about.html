About me · the story behind the flashcards

Hi, I’m Shine! Welcome.

This website is designed for anyone with an appetite for foreign language and looking to learn Traditional Chinese.

Food words seemed like an easy gateway to learning how to read a foreign language, given its versatility.

Why I built this

Perhaps to clarify, as a Canadian foreigner of Hong Kong descent living in this special administrative region for 6+ years now, I designed and created this website with the goal of brushing up my language skills. My spoken Cantonese and Mandarin are adequate, but in order to reach another level of fluency, I believe that being able to write and read is important.

Growing up in Canada, I never learned how to read Chinese, despite being able to speak Cantonese.

If you’re looking to pick up another skill, join me on my quest to perfect my Chinese and check out Flashcards!
